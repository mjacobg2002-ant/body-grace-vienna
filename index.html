Meet Paula

Body Grace
became personal.

Paula came to yoga after nearly twenty years of long-distance running and a career coaching basketball — including seasons spent coaching abroad.

When a diagnosis of arthritis and osteopenia arrived, her doctor encouraged her toward yoga to build joint fluidity and bone strength. That first step led her deep into breath, anatomy, and eventually teacher training under Body Grace's founder, Jan Crerie.

In 2019, when Body Grace was at risk of closing, Paula and her husband Michael made a decision: they would keep this community going. They took over the studio — not as a business, but as a place worth protecting.

Her philosophy is simple: you are welcome here.
