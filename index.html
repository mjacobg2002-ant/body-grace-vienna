Our Story

Meet Paula.

Paula came to yoga after nearly twenty years of running and coaching — and stayed for what it gave back to her.

In 2019, when Body Grace was at risk of closing, she and her husband Michael chose to keep this community going. They took over the studio — not as a business, but as a place worth protecting.

Her philosophy is simple: you are welcome here.
